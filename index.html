From farm to pack

Our planned production flow.

  1. 1

    Source

    Soybeans and spices from local farmers and traders.

  2. 2

    Extract

    Crude oil extracted from soybean seed.

  3. 3

    Refine

    Oil refined and purified.

  4. 4

    Process

    Spices cleaned, dried, ground and blended.

  5. 5

    Pack

    Sachets, bottles, tins and spice packs.

  6. 6

    Deliver

    Branding and distribution to markets.

Business highlights

  • Soybean oil extraction, refining and packaging
  • Spice cleaning, drying, grinding, blending and packing
  • Branding, packaging and distribution of food products
  • Soybean meal supplied to the animal feed industry
