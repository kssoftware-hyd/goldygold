About Us

Patel Oil And Food Process Company Pvt. Ltd.

Company overview

Our primary objective is to produce refined edible oil from soybeans, manufacture a variety of edible spices, and provide local employment. We plan to make high-quality edible products and supply them to the local market, Maharashtra and other states.

Our fields of work are edible oil production, edible spice manufacturing and food processing.

WhatsApp enquiry

What we plan to make

Edible oil

Crude oil extraction from soybean seeds, refining and purification, and packing in sachets, bottles and tins. Other edible oils may follow.

Edible spices

Turmeric, red chili, coriander, garam masala, chicken masala, biryani masala, veg masala, pav bhaji masala, kala masala and other blends.

By-products

Soybean meal for the animal feed industry, plus branding, packaging and distribution.

Mission

To add value to local agricultural produce by making quality, safe edible oils and spices, and to create jobs for local youth and women.

Vision

To contribute to the industrial and economic development of our region and expand into new markets as production capacity grows.

Our commitments

  • Quality and safe products
  • Collaboration with farmers, traders and small businesses
  • Employment in production, packing, quality control, warehousing, transport, sales and office roles
  • Customer satisfaction and reliable supply
